🏔️ Side Quest: Sunrise Hiker

Mountains I've Conquered

There's something about waking up before dawn, hiking in the dark, and watching the world wake up from the summit. Here are three mountains that have become part of my story:

Cerro Chirripó

The highest point in Costa Rica. A demanding multi-kilometer hike that passes through páramo landscapes, cloud forests, and views that feel almost otherworldly.

Volcán Arenal

An iconic volcano with an almost perfectly symmetrical shape. The hike around its base combines tropical rainforest, abundant wildlife, and views of old lava flows.

Cerro Pelado

A mountain known for its strong winds and spectacular sunsets. The climb is short but steep, and the panoramic view at the top is incredible.
