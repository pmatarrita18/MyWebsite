COMPLETED

Sky Free - Reimagined

How could you practice skiing, without going out?

Built for the Electronics as Materials class at CIID. We created an artifact that lets you play the classic game SkiFree using physical movement—no keyboard needed.

Using simple materials (wooden planks, marbles, and copper), we built a circuit that detects sliding motion. Tilt the planks like you're skiing, and watch your character carve down the slopes on screen. Physical play meets digital nostalgia.
